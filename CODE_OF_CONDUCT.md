# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful place for learning, reviewing code, and improving software. Participation is expected to be free from harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and disclosure of another person's private information.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with evidence and respect.
- Welcome questions from contributors at different experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback about behavior, not people.
- Protect credentials, customer data, and private operational details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or sexualized language.
- Spam, promotion unrelated to the project, or deliberately misleading reports.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or security-sensitive details without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer through a private GitHub contact channel. Do not include sensitive information in public issues. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content; warn participants; or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
